Mission & vision

Why we exist

Our mission
To rescue children and adolescents from the streets, abandonment, abuse and exploitation by giving them the opportunity to restore their lives through a program that educates and trains them from a Christian worldview.
Our vision
Transforming families one child at a time.

The Boys' Farm

A place where they can be a kid

Many of our boys have already lost important relationships and opportunities at an early age. They come from places where one or both parents have been taken by poverty, drugs, prostitution, crime, incarceration or death.

Most come from the roughest neighborhoods of Medellín. The Boys' Farm is a 45-acre dairy ranch about 45 minutes outside the city. Here the children can be at ease, rest and have the chance to be a child again.

Our goal is that every boy at the farm finds a new life in Christ, with life-transforming opportunities in a safe, loving, Christ-centered environment.
