Child sponsorship

Change a child’s life for about $1 a day.

For $30 a month you can help us change the lives of precious children. As a sponsor you’ll receive our monthly Child Sponsorship Newsletter, which introduces a different child each month.

What your sponsorship provides

Every month, your $30 goes toward

Shelter & provision

A safe place to grow up, far from the danger, violence and drugs the children are used to seeing on the streets, on a 45-acre cattle farm on the outskirts of Medellín.

Education

While they live with us, the children are enrolled in the local school. Many arrive several grade levels behind where a child their age should be.

The Gospel

Most importantly, the children have the opportunity to learn about the Gospel and the love our Heavenly Father has for us.
