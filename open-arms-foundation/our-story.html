Our story

How it all started

Open Arms Foundation was conceived in 1991 by missionaries William and Wanda Perrow in response to the abandonment, violence, abuse and neglect of children living on the streets of Medellín, Colombia.

These children were primarily victims of poverty, prostitution, alcohol and drugs. Many sniffed industrial glue to quiet their hunger and numb themselves to the atrocities that defined their reality. Often considered beyond repair, they were known as “throw-aways,” broken by the emotional, physical and sexual abuse they had endured.

God reminded Bill that just as Christ had rescued him from the damage of drug addiction, He could transform the lives of these children too.

With other volunteers and missionaries, the Perrows took to the streets of Colombia, looking past the dirt, the tattered clothes and society’s indifference to reach these forgotten faces. Through Open Arms they remain committed to feeding, clothing, sheltering and restoring these children through the love and transforming power of Jesus Christ.
